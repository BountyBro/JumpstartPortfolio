User's Perspective

How Does Jumpstart Affect You?

What is Jumpstart?

Jumpstart is a hobby planning application built to be offline, private, and robust. Utilizing a large language model (LLM) built on a Microsoft-curated dataset, Jumpstart turns ideas into scheduled events with step-by-step procedures by creating plans based on your interests and collaborating with you to find a plan that fits you.

  • Offline: Jumpstart operates fully offline; no connection required. LLMs are run locally, so performance varies per device and in your control.
  • Private: Your data never leaves your device. All user data is opt-in with provided use cases to clarify impact on the application. User data is easily identifiable, accessible, and can be deleted at any time.
  • Robust: Jumpstart is designed modularly, enabling you to add your LLM of choice as a .gguf file and toggle extensions to control Jumpstart's capabilities.

How Jumpstart Works

By assessing your interests, budget, and schedule, Jumpstart can draft plans that fit you. Further details are clarified in a back-and-forth conversation with an LLM to fine tune additional details or provide suggestions.

  1. Outline Your Details: On first running Jumpstart, you will be asked to complete a survey to determine your interests, budget, and schedule to be applied across all plans and suggestions.
  2. Pose An Idea: To get a plan rolling, pose an idea such as "I want to learn how to knit. What would be a good first project?". From this, Jumpstart will provide a fully-detailed event plan with a date, duration, budget, and steps.
  3. Refine Your Plan: If the plan doesn't fit just right, change it! Changes can be made directly, like asking to change from knitting a sweater to knitting gloves, or indirectly by asking for alternatives.

Why Use Jumpstart?

As a busy person, you may find yourself procrastinating in pursuing your hobbies. With Jumpstart, you can jump straight into your hobbies without having to spend hours scheduling, budgeting, and planning. Just open Jumpstart, describe what you want to do, and fine tune your new plan for a fine afternoon. Unlike Voiset, a general-purpose AI planner app, Jumpstart is free to use, operates offline, and private.
