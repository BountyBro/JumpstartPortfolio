Jumpstart

The Hobby Planning Platform

Burnout is a common symptom of a working life. Often times, you may get stuck in a cycle of work, recover, and repeat; however, this creates an absence of what matters most: what you want to do.

Planning what you want to do takes time and energy, of which there is little left after long days. With Jumpstart, you can plan hobbies new and old in less than a minute.

Jumpstart utilizes a compact, locally-run LLM to suggest hobby plans based on your needs, without selling your data or consuming clean water resevoirs.

Your hobbies. Your data. Your way.
